ABOUT THE DATA

Real records. Clear definitions.

This site brings together historical prices from public reports, not live market quotes. Percentages on product cards compare the first and last observations shown. They are not daily changes or popularity rankings.

Memory and flash prices refer to upstream chips and wafer quotation categories. Oil and gold use the market benchmarks described for each product. These are not retail store prices, and values across products are not directly comparable.

City prices are illustrative examples for demonstrating regional switching, not observed retail quotes. Global charts remain independent of the selected city. Curves are visual guides between observations, not additional measurements. Charts connect verified observations only; the lines do not represent additional measurements. Price dates and publication dates are shown separately. Refer to the original source for precise timing. News summaries describe reported influences, not proof of a single cause.

Every price point includes a source link. Your followed products are saved only in this browser. This historical edition does not update automatically.
