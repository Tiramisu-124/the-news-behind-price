# Team content guide

Edit editorial-data.js to replace the five product stories. Keep each id unchanged. Fields: category, name, unit, sydney/melbourne (four illustrative prices), source (original report URL), publisher, headline, summary (source facts), steps (three transmission stages), analysis (your interpretation), other (alternative explanations), evidence (reporting still required).

All current city prices and trend charts are fictional coursework examples. News links are real; they do not substantiate those prices. Existing global benchmarks are separate from these examples.

Before publishing real research: add dated observations, retailer/source links, exact product specifications, price currency/unit, report publication date, author and review date. Update editorial.js to render these fields and remove demo labels only for verified entries. Do not simply replace numbers and imply they are verified. Keep missing cities explicitly unavailable rather than inventing comparisons.

For shuttlecocks compare the same brand, feather grade and tube size. For petrol distinguish crude oil from pump petrol. Gold city comparisons are dealer quotes, not city spot prices. RAM must use a matched SKU. Coffee must match cup size and milk type.

Preserve original reporting vs your causal interpretation as separate sections. Temporal correlation alone does not establish causation.
